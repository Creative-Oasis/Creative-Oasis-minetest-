How to use Moving Blocks?

1) Build whatever you like using the mod's blocks (or blocks with "(M)" in their name).
2) Place a generator (hydrogen generator (details below) or fuel generator).
3) Connect the generator and the pusher block using MovingBlocks cables.
4) Activate the generators (by adding hydrogen or fuel).
5) Click the pusher block to move the structure (the direction is giving by the kind of arrow at the top of the block)
Optional: You can connect the fuel generator to pipes and a chimney to make the chimney work.

How to use hydrogen in the mod?

1) Build a hydrogen generator and place it near a water source.
2.1) If "technic" mod is enabled: connect an MV cable to the hydrogen creator to produce hydrogen.
2.2) Else: place the hydrogen generator in a well-lit area to produce hydrogen.
3) Place hydrogen into the hydrogen generator to produce energy (each machine operation consumes one unit of hydrogen from each generator).
Warning) When hydrogen is stored in tanks, a generator, or a creator, it can explode if exposed to nearby fire or explosions.
Optional) Hydrogen tanks can be moved by a Moving Block machine, allowing them to serve as onboard fuel tanks.

Tips:
If you hit the fuel generator while holding fuel, the item will be added to the generator.
The same applies to hydrogen-related items (additionally, hitting a hydrogen block with your bare hands retrieves the hydrogen contained within the block).
There is no size limit for builds, so you can create large vessels. However, a massive machine could slow down the game (lag).
