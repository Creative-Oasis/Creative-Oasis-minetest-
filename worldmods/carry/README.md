# Carry Mod for Minetest

Carry your friends around like a boss 😎  

## How to use

- **Pick up a player:** Crouch + right-click  
- **Drop them:** Crouch + right-click again  

When you carry someone, they can’t move or jump, they just follow you. Your arms move a little so it looks natural.  

## Features

- Smoothly attaches the player to you  
- Works with any player  
- Automatically drops players if they leave the game  

## Installation

1. Put the mod folder in your `mods`  
2. Enable it in your world  
3. Go carry your friends around 😏  

Simple, fun, done. Go mess around and test it out 🔥
