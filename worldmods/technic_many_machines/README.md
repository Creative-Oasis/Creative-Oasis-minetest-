# technic_many_machines
Hopefully add many technic machines

## Machines
This is just an overview of what machines are contained in this mod.
### Cobblestone Generators
They come in LV, MV, and HV forms.

### Drop Extractors
A useful machine that can get all the drops from a node. Eg 1 gravel -> 1 flint.

They come in LV, MV, HV forms.

### LV Moving Walkway
Slowly moves the player above.

### MV Crusher
Can get materials from certain items, and can also recycle tools.

Crushed items can also give metal pieces.

### HV Sawmill
Can get 8 wood from most trees

### HV Advanced Alloy Furnace
A very important machine, for the making of new alloys. Many machines require its alloys.

### HV Bulk Compressor
Capable of compressing items in bulk, as well as having some new recipes. You will need this.

### HV Bulk Alloy Furnace
6 times faster than a Mv Alloy Furnace

### HV Radiation Chamber
Can take 10 uranium dust of any kind, and get thorium out of it.

The higher the fissile, the faster it goes.

Thorium is only used in the Asteroid teleporter as of now.
### HV Asteroid Teleporter
A very expensive multiblock machine that teleports segments of asteroids into its interior. Recommended to be used with a HV quarry connected to a luacontroller via digilines.

### HV Forge
An very expensive multiblock used to make MK2 versions of existing machines. MK2 machines are much stronger versions of their base counterpart.
